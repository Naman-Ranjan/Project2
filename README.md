# 📊 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing customer shopping behavior to identify purchasing patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and opportunities for improving customer retention and revenue.

The project uses **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Jupyter Notebook
* SQL
* MySQL
* Power BI

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Data Cleaning → Exploratory Data Analysis → SQL Analysis → Data Visualization → Business Insights**

### Python — Data Preparation & EDA

* Cleaned and transformed raw customer shopping data using Pandas.
* Performed exploratory data analysis to understand customer and purchasing patterns.
* Prepared the dataset for SQL-based business analysis.

### SQL — Business Analysis

* Analyzed customer purchasing behavior and product performance using SQL.
* Performed customer segmentation and revenue analysis.
* Used **JOINs, GROUP BY, CASE statements, aggregate functions, subqueries, CTEs, and window functions** to answer business questions.

### Power BI — Dashboard

* Built an interactive dashboard to visualize customer behavior and purchasing trends.
* Created KPIs and visualizations for customer segments, products, categories, demographics, and purchasing behavior.
* Added interactive filters to enable business-focused exploration of the data.

## 📈 Key Insights

* Identified purchasing patterns across different customer segments and demographics.
* Analyzed revenue contribution across products and customer groups.
* Identified high-value customer segments and potential retention opportunities.
* Evaluated product and category performance to identify areas of higher demand.
* Translated analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Develop targeted retention strategies for high-value customers.
* Use customer segmentation to personalize marketing campaigns.
* Focus promotions on high-performing products and categories.
* Encourage repeat purchases through personalized offers and loyalty initiatives.
